_Farthing_ is the first in Walton's Small Changes sequence, an old-school crime novel, set in an alternate Britain of the 1940s.

_Ha'penny_, the next in the sequence, tied for the 2008 Prometheus Award. A first-class retro thriller, with a plot to assassinate Hitler and the quisling British prime minister, it has much understated resonance with the contemporary world.

Both novels were highly recommended by a 2008 poster to the original Anarchy-SF mailing list.
