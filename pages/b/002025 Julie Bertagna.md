Set in 2099, when almost all land has been drowned. A small island is evacuated, the survivors encountering new cities towering above the sea, the exclusive refuge of the wealthy, with an underclass barely subsisting, and what remains of Glasgow lying below; rebellion ensues, and a new exodus begins.

This YA novel is mentioned as an example of 'disaster utopianism', in Rhiannon Firth's afterword to the 2019 edition of Marie Louise Berneri's _Journey Through Utopia_.
