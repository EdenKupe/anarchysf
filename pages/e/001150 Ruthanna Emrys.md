In a future North America where the nation state has all but withered away, and though corporations still hold sway much of society is now organised around the maintenance of river watersheds, an alien spacecraft 
lands and first contact is made with an ordinary woman from one of the watersheds. The story follows through to a hopeful conclusion of co-operation between humans and aliens.

The book is the subject of a long review by <a href="https://www.anarchistfederation.net/a-half-built-garden-by-ruthanna-emrys-tor-2022/#/">Kevin Carson</a>, originally published by the Center for a Stateless Society, and reprinted by the Anarchist Federation. Carson says "If you’re a fan of fiction set in a better world the day after tomorrow, as an escape or source of inspiration in the face of an increasingly scary tomorrow, I highly recommend this book."

Also recommended by Paul Watson on his <a href="https://www.lazaruscorporation.co.uk/blogs/artists-notebook/posts/anarchism-in-speculative-fiction">Anarchism and Speculative Fiction</a> page.
